Nyaya Sathi — Major Project

AI Legal Assistant · Agentic RAG · Multilingual

A web-based AI legal assistant for India that answers legal questions in Hindi, English and Kannada, drafts ready-to-use legal documents, and supports voice input with spoken replies.

The problem: legal information in India is hard to navigate and heavily English-centric. Nyaya Sathi gives citizens plain-language guidance in their own language — and gives lawyers cited case-law research.

Key features

  • Agentic RAG chat over Indian laws & case judgments
  • Citizen and Lawyer modes — lawyer mode adds cited case-law research
  • Legal document generation (legal notice, RTI, rent agreement, affidavit & more) as DOCX + PDF
  • Voice input and read-aloud replies via Sarvam STT/TTS
  • Streaming answers over a FastAPI backend; history kept in the browser
  • Multilingual: Hindi, English and Kannada
